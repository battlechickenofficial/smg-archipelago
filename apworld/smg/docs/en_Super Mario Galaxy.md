# Super Mario Galaxy

## Where is the options page?

The player options page for this game contains all the options you need to configure and export a config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Every Power Star is a location: collecting it sends an item to someone in the multiworld. Galaxies and Luigi are
items. Collecting stars never opens a galaxy for you; another player has to find its unlock item.

Optionally, Star Shuffle swaps which mission each star select entry plays, either inside each galaxy or across the
whole game.

## What is the goal?

Beat Bowser in Bowser's Galaxy Reactor. The galaxy needs its unlock item and a number of Power Stars (an option,
60 by default). With "Unlock Everything on Goal", every galaxy and Luigi open in your game afterwards.

## What items are there?

- One unlock item per galaxy (except Gateway and the starting galaxy).
- Luigi, which lets you play as Luigi from the file select.
- Filler: 1-Up Mushrooms, star bits, coins, life refills, and power-ups (Fire Flower, Ice Flower, Bee, Spring and
  Boo Mushrooms, Red Star, Rainbow Star). Power-ups can carry over between levels and need not run out.
- Optional traps, each switched on or off: damage, power-up loss, star bit drain, and enemy traps that drop a group
  of enemies on top of you (which enemies and how many is up to you).

## What does another world's item look like in Super Mario Galaxy?

The star is collected as usual; a message names the item and who it went to.

## Is there anything the mod does on its own?

No. The mod only does what the multiworld's slot data tells it. Without a connected (or previously connected)
Archipelago seed, the game plays exactly as normal.
